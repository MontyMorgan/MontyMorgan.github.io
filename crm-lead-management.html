THE APPROACH

A clear process.
Thoughtful execution.

01 /

Keep contact records useful

Capture names, contact information, lead source, and relevant conversation details. Check existing records before adding a contact to reduce duplicates.

02 /

Use consistent lead stages

Use agreed stages to show where a lead stands. Update the record after meaningful activity so the team can understand progress without piecing together old messages.

03 /

Plan the next follow-up

Create a task with a realistic date and purpose after each conversation. Use the contact’s preferences and agreed timing to guide outreach.

04 /

Write clear activity notes

Log what was discussed, what matters to the contact, and what was promised. Keep notes factual and concise so another team member can pick up the conversation.

05 /

Review the pipeline

Look for overdue tasks, missing details, and leads without a next step. Prioritize using the team’s goals and each lead’s actual engagement.

06 /

Check workflows carefully

When using existing reminders or automations, verify the intended trigger and outcome with a test record. Keep contact preferences and the team’s process in view.
