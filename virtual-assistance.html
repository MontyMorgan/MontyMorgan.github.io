THE APPROACH

A clear process.
Thoughtful execution.

01 /

Clarify priorities

Confirm the task, expected outcome, deadline, and available resources. Ask focused questions when missing details could affect the result.

02 /

Organize the work

Break larger assignments into manageable actions. Track due dates and dependencies so important steps are visible and easier to coordinate.

03 /

Keep communication useful

Share concise progress updates, flag blockers early, and make the next action clear. Match the client’s preferred communication style.

04 /

Coordinate the details

Support scheduling, task handoffs, follow-ups, and document organization. Check details such as dates and time zones before confirming arrangements.

05 /

Protect accuracy and access

Work within the access provided, double-check important information, and keep client materials organized. Request missing permissions when a task needs them.

06 /

Close the loop

Review the output against the request, share the completed work, and note anything still pending. Keep recurring task instructions current as the process develops.
