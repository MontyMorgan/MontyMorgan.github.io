THE APPROACH

A clear process.
Thoughtful execution.

01 /

Research before the call

Start with the audience, their business, and the likely reason they might need support. Keep the opening relevant to their situation rather than reciting a generic pitch.

02 /

Open with a clear purpose

Introduce yourself, explain the reason for calling in a short sentence, and check whether it is a suitable time to speak. Give the person room to respond.

03 /

Ask, listen, and qualify

Use open questions to understand the current process, challenges, priorities, and timing. Listen carefully and summarize what you heard before suggesting a next step.

04 /

Handle objections with curiosity

Acknowledge the concern and ask a focused question to understand it. Explain only what is relevant, avoid pressure, and respect a clear no.

05 /

Agree on the next step

End a productive conversation with one clear action, such as sharing information or arranging a meeting. Confirm the details and follow through as agreed.

06 /

Review and improve

Record useful notes and review recurring questions and objections. Refine the approach using actual conversations and feedback rather than assuming one script fits everyone.
